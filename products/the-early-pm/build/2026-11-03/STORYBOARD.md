## Video direction

- **Palette:** broadside (frame.md). Two registers only: dark (ink-black ground, cream text, fire-orange accent) and orange (fire-orange ground, ink text). Frames 1, 3, 4, 6 dark; frames 2 and 5 orange, so the register flip marks the reassure beat and the takeaway.
- **Type:** Barlow display lowercase 900 as the hero, IBM Plex Mono uppercase for kickers and source lines. On-screen copy is the script's on-screen text, kept word for word (numbers exactly "15.7%", "~15,000", "45 MIN").
- **Safe area (load-bearing):** all frame content inside x 80–850, y 300–1150 of the 1080x1920 canvas (4:5 grid crop + Instagram UI zones; right 230 px clear). Captions sit in a band at y ≈ 1180–1420, so nothing from frames goes below y 1150. Grounds may be full-bleed.
- **Motion grammar:** hard, poster-like. Text slams in (fast scale-down from 1.15 + opacity, power3/expo out), reveals paced to the voiceover cues. Holds are still; at most a tiny jitter. No floating, no drift.
- **Rhythm:** frame 1 slams at frame 0 (hook up at frame 1). Frame 5 is the held breather before the CTA. Frame 3 is the one dense frame (stat).
- **Negative list:** no AI gradients, no bokeh, no icons of robots/brains, no logos of Cursor/Bolt/Lovable (text only), no screen recordings, no slideshow (front-load then freeze), no screensaver drift.
