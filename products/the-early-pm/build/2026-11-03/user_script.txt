AI PM doesn't mean managing AI products.
If you're early in PM, that title is scaring you for nothing.
One analysis of about 15,000 PM job posts in May 2026 found 15.7% asked for generative AI skills. That's about one in six.
And the ask is often practical. Some interviews have you build a prototype in 45 minutes with tools like Cursor, Bolt or Lovable.
So the skill is using AI to build, not running a model team.
