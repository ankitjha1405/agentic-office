# The Early PM · Instagram Landscape Research

**Task:** MKT-1 · **Author:** Aakash Tiwari · **Accessed:** all sources 2026-10-29 unless stated.
**Research question:** Who already owns product-management attention on Instagram, what do they post, and where can a faceless channel for early-career PMs, AI PMs and PM builders win?

**Labels:** `[F]` fact (source cited) · `[E]` estimate (derived from facts, reasoning shown) · `[H]` hypothesis (untested).

## 0. Read this first: where the data is thin

- **No public engagement data was retrievable.** Instagram's unauthenticated profile pages show followers, bio and post dates/types only, not likes, views or captions. Third-party stats sites returned errors or irrelevant results. So "what works" (section 2) is inferred from account size, cadence and format mix, not from per-post views. Treat it as `[E]`/`[H]`. `[F]`
- **No per-post permalinks.** Post-level links are not exposed without login. "Standout posts" are therefore the observed on-screen text of posts, with the profile link. Before the first post, someone with an Instagram login should pull view/like counts from the profiles below (a 30-minute manual task, no scraping).
- **Search tooling was weak for this niche.** Several searches returned nothing relevant (queries listed in section 7). Accounts below were found mostly by restricting search to instagram.com. The list is likely missing some mid-size PM creators, especially Indian ones.
- **Follower counts are as shown on the public profile page on 2026-10-29.** Very low counts on big brands (for example @tryexponent: 27) look like a stale or partial page render, not truth. Marked "unreliable".

## 5. Top 3 recommendations

### R1. Positioning: "The PM who ships, for people early in the PM career"
One line `[H]`: **"Learn product management by watching someone build and ship it, no fluff, no face."** Own the intersection of early-career PM, AI tools and building, where only tiny accounts sit today. Faceless works because the "face" is the product/prototype on screen.

### R2. Content pillars (4)
1. **Ship it** (PM builders): screen-recorded builds, prompts, prototypes, before/after.
2. **First 90 days** (early-career reality): scenarios, mistakes, stakeholder situations, what to do.
3. **AI PM, decoded:** one AI concept or evals/spec lesson per post, in plain language.
4. **PM life, drawn** (humour, minority share ~15%): relatable memes in a consistent designed style, for reach/shares.
Optional later: India lens as a series, not a pillar, until confirmed.

### R3. Format mix for a faceless account (starting hypothesis, then adjust from Insights `[H]`)
- ~50% **reels**: screen recordings, text-on-screen + voiceover (TTS or recorded voice, not face), 15-45 s, hook in 1-2 s. Use Trial Reels to test hooks.
- ~35% **carousels**: step-by-step, "mistakes", templates, saved as reference. Add audio so they can reach the Reels tab.
- ~15% **memes/static**: shareable, low effort.
- Cadence: 4-5 posts/week. Reason `[E]`: top accounts post 3-7/week; consistency alone did not win for small accounts, so quality of hook matters more than volume.
- Review at 30 days: replace pillars using Insights (saves, shares, non-follower reach).

### 10 sample post ideas
1. Reel: "I built a working app prototype as a PM in 10 minutes. Here's the prompt." (Ship it)
2. Carousel: "5 things I'd never ask engineering until I'd prototyped them." (Ship it)
3. Reel: "PM vs AI coding tool: what it got wrong in round one." (Ship it, screen recording)
4. Carousel: "Your first 90 days as a new PM, week by week." (First 90 days)
5. Reel: "The meeting where my first roadmap got torn apart, and what I'd do now." (First 90 days)
6. Carousel: "What is an eval? AI PM jargon in 6 slides." (AI PM)
7. Reel: "Write a PRD for an AI feature: the 4 questions normal PRDs skip." (AI PM)
8. Meme: "Stakeholder: 'Quick change.' Engineering: [image]." in the channel's house style. (PM life)
9. Carousel: "PM resume line, before and after: from task list to impact." (First 90 days / light interview)
10. Reel: "Hot take: you don't need to learn to code. You need to learn to build." (Ship it, opinion hook)
