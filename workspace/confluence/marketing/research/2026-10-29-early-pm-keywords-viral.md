# The Early PM · Keywords, Viral Swipe File and What To Create

**Task:** MKT-2 (follow-up to MKT-1) · **Author:** Aakash Tiwari · **All sources accessed 2026-10-29.**
Labels: `[F]` fact (source cited) · `[E]` estimate (reasoned from facts) · `[H]` hypothesis.

## Verdict first (the decision)

**Build C, but weighted: a career-first page with a builder edge. "Get hired as a PM by proving you can build." Roughly 60% career hooks, 40% AI-builder proof.** `[H]`

Why, in six lines:
1. **Small PM-career accounts have real outliers on Instagram right now.** @akad_technology (3.6K followers) hit 286K views on "top US PM pay by company, per Levels.fyi", about 80x its followers. @geekyballer.ai (20.2K) hit 318K on PM career levels (16x). @uxr.pri (8.2K) hit 150K on "Being an AI PM doesn't mean managing AI products" (18x). `[F]` for the numbers, `[E]` for the multiples (views divided by followers, see method note).
2. **Pure AI-builder content is huge but is a different, far more crowded audience.** #aitools shows 7.3M reels, #claudecode 254K, #buildwithai 135K, #vibecoding 63K. In #buildwithai, about 9 of the 12 top posts use the same "comment a keyword for the link" mechanic. `[F]` Those viewers are creators, freelancers and hobbyists, not PMs. `[E]`
3. **The builder topic is cooling in search.** US Google searches for "vibe coding" peaked about 165K/month in March 2026 and were about 110K in June 2026 (Keyword Planner via DataForSEO); "vibe coding cursor" is down about 82% year over year. `[F]` single secondary source.
4. **But building is becoming a PM hiring requirement,** which is exactly the early-career PM's anxiety. Secondary sources say PMs are asked to build prototypes in 45 minutes with Cursor, Bolt or Lovable, and about 15.7% of roughly 15,000 PM job posts (May 2026) require generative-AI skills. `[F]` secondary, job-board analysis, treat as indicative.
5. **The overlap, "PM who builds", has almost no one on Instagram.** In MKT-1 the only direct account was @productmanagerhub (3.4K followers, static jokes). No account in either hashtag's top 12 does early-career PM plus building. `[E]` (limited by tooling).
6. **Faceless fit:** both the money/levels hooks and the build demos work as text cards plus screen recordings. 

Why the others lose:
- **A (pure career) loses** because the top-of-funnel hooks (interviews, resumes, generic salary) are the most copied and face-led, and entry-level PM hiring is tight, so "how to get a PM job" alone gives no new angle. Highest demand, least differentiation. Interview prep already has 3+ dedicated accounts plus Product School (MKT-1). `[E]`
- **B (pure builder) loses** because it competes with 100K-700K follower creators doing the same demos (@nick_saraev 726K, @rajenlabs 184K, @scalewithimanshu 401K), the formula is cooling, the audience is not PMs, and there is no "who is this for" reason to follow a nameless page. `[E]`

## Method note and data limits (read this)

- **Method:** from the `ig-viral` skill: rank by how far a post beats its own account. The skill uses views divided by the account's own median reel. **I could not get account medians** (Instagram profile pages show no view counts to a logged-out reader). I use **views ÷ followers as a proxy ("x followers")**. Treat 3x+ as a signal, 10x+ as strong. A big account can score low simply because its followers are not all active, so the proxy is rough. `[E]`
- **Where the numbers come from:** Instagram's public hashtag pages (`instagram.com/explore/tags/<tag>/`) show the top 12 reels with view counts, and public profile pages show follower counts. No login used, no scraping at volume, about 50 page reads in total. Hashtag "top" lists are chosen by Instagram and are a snapshot, not a ranking of all posts. `[F]`
- **No post permalinks.** Logged-out pages expose only the account, the views and a text summary. Links below are the hashtag page where the post appeared plus the account profile. A person with login can open the account and find the post by the hook text.
- **Google Trends** was not retrievable. Demand signals are: hashtag reel counts shown on the page, the top-post view ceiling, and secondary Keyword Planner figures. Where a hashtag page needed login (#productmanagerinterview, #productmanagementcareer, #lovableai count), I say so.
- **YouTube/TikTok view counts:** searches did not return usable Shorts or TikTok data. Only long-form: Starter Story "I Built an App with Cursor, Made $30K, and Quit My Job" about 694K views and a Claude-built-app video about 493K (via [rosetta.to](https://rosetta.to/u/starterstory/i-built-an-app-with-cursor-made-30k-and-quit-my-job?t=0), `[F]`, indicative only). Reddit: only mirror snapshots, no direct threads, so Reddit is weak here.
- Face vs faceless is inferred from auto-generated alt text only, so it is `[H]` throughout.

## 4. Verdict, risks and kill-switch

**C (career-first, builder edge)**, reasoning in the opening section.
Content split `[H]`: 6 of 10 first posts career/money/level hooks, 4 builder/prototype proofs.

Risks:
1. **Proxy metric:** outliers use followers, not account medians. One-off viral posts (@ai.india.pro 2.3M, @rajenlabs 57M) may be luck. Do not plan on their scale. `[E]`
2. **Thin PM-specific reach:** PM hashtags have low top-post ceilings versus AI hashtags. Builder hooks supply reach, career hooks supply the right audience. `[E]`
3. **Builder topic cooling:** vibe-coding search interest is down from the March 2026 peak (one secondary source). Mitigation: sell "PM proof of work", not "vibe coding". `[E]`
4. **Inflated claims:** salary and AI-PM claims often unverified. Use sourced figures only (Levels.fyi, job-post analyses) with the source named on screen. `[H]`
5. **Faceless authority gap:** accounts with strong PM outliers often cite credentials (ex-Amazon, ex-Microsoft, Head of AI Products). A nameless page needs a proof device: real sources on screen, real builds, a consistent character or voice. `[H]`
6. **Platform rules:** comment-keyword giveaways and automated DMs can trigger spam limits. Prepare, do not automate. `[H]`

Kill-switch (test, `[H]`, revised 2026-10-29 by Ankit after CEO input): the account starts with zero real followers (current followers are paid), so follower multiples are meaningless. Judge the first 20 posts on absolute numbers from Insights instead:
- Views per reel at 7 days: median of at least 500, and at least 3 reels above 5,000.
- Follows per 1,000 views: at least 3 (shows the topic converts, not just gets watched).
- Shares plus saves per 1,000 views: at least 10.
- Compare the career half against the builder half on these numbers. If builder posts beat career posts on two of the three, move the mix to 40/60 and re-run this research.
Paid followers do not watch or engage, so they drag the account's engagement rate down. Treat them as noise, ideally remove them, and never buy more. Use Trial Reels (shown only to non-followers) to test hooks.
