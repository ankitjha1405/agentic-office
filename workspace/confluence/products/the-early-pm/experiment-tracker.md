**Task:** MKT-3 - Fill in from Instagram Insights 7 days after each post. Log views from non-followers only where Insights splits it. Posts are in `week-1-posts.md`.

## Kill-switch targets (MKT-2, revised 2026-10-29), judged over the first 20 posts

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Views per reel at 7 days | Median at least 500, and at least 3 reels above 5,000 |
| Follows per 1,000 views | At least 3 |
| Shares + saves per 1,000 views | At least 10 |
| Career vs builder | If builder beats career on 2 of 3 metrics, move the mix to 40/60 and re-run the research |

Formulas: follows/1K = follows ÷ views × 1,000. Shares+saves/1K = (shares + saves) ÷ views × 1,000. Carousel "views" = Insights views. Existing followers are paid and do not engage, so ignore follower count and engagement rate.

## Verdict key

- **Keep:** passes at least 2 of the 3 targets. Make a follow-up.
- **Tweak:** passes 1. Change the hook or the first card, repost in 2 weeks.
- **Kill:** passes 0. Drop the format or topic.
